Why the order matters

The existential design was already explicit in January. May sharpened the same model from another angle: independent effect identities, matching resets, and effect lifting without making “effect” a privileged kind in the core language.

The async thread predates the existential story. It supplied the runtime and typing constraint that later prevents unsound effect erasure: a continuation may itself be the suspended capability.

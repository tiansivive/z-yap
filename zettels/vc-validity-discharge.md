# VC validity discharge

VC validity discharge is the layer between Yap-generated IVL formulas and the raw satisfiability solver.

The layer treats a VC as a proof obligation. It structurally traverses conjunctions and guarded universals, accumulates an environment of symbolic binders plus path assumptions, and discharges leaf goals by checking whether the accumulated assumptions together with the negated goal are satisfiable.

For a guarded shape:

```text
forall x. guard(x) => goal(x)
```

the discharger proves `goal(x)` under an environment extended with symbolic `x` and assumption `guard(x)`. At the leaf it asks the solver for:

```text
check-sat(env_assumptions and not goal)
```

The result mapping is verifier-facing: raw `unsat` becomes `valid`; raw `sat` becomes `invalid` with a counterexample model; raw `unknown` stays unknown.

This is not a new theory solver. It is a polarity and structure layer that preserves the Liquid VC discipline before handing quantifier-free leaves to CDCL(T). General quantified SMT machinery remains useful for backend robustness, but nested guarded VCs generated by Yap should usually be consumed here rather than by full MBQI.

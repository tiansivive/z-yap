# Cong and Asai — handling delimited continuations with dependent types

Reference for integrating shift/reset-style control operators with dependent typing. The relevant pressure for Yap is the interaction between captured continuations, dependency in types, answer-type behavior, and preservation of soundness when control effects appear in a dependently typed language.
